Tools for the work behind the paper.

Browser-based research utilities for exploratory data analysis, manuscript-based journal discovery, and manuscript structure. Your inputs stay in this browser unless you export them.

Data Analysis Lab

A guided, reproducible workflow for CSV and Excel research data. Start by checking the structure and variable types, document filters and exclusions, explore distributions, choose analyses that match your design, inspect diagnostics, then export figures and an audit record. Browser-based calculations are exploratory aids; verify assumptions and report methods transparently.
Recommended sequence: validate units and coding → inspect missing/duplicate values → apply and record justified exclusions → inspect distributions → fit the chosen model/test → review assumptions and uncertainty → export results. Do not remove outliers solely because they look unusual.

Find journals from your manuscript

Paste a manuscript title and/or abstract. The finder searches OpenAlex for related published works and identifies journals that published those works. Results include source links and example papers. Ranking is based on retrieved evidence, not acceptance probability. No quartiles, indexing claims, APCs, or acceptance rates are guessed.
Paste your title or abstract to begin. Search runs against public OpenAlex publication metadata.

Important: similar-paper venues are candidates, not a guarantee of scope fit. Verify the journal’s current aims and scope, article type, Scopus/Web of Science coverage, quartile and APC on authoritative sources before submission.

Manuscript Structure Workspace

Inspect TXT or DOCX structure before submission. The workspace checks section coverage and order, abstract length, keyword presence, figure/table captions, citation markers, references heading, repeated headings and likely cross-reference gaps. Results are automated screening indicators—not editorial decisions or proof that citations are correct.
