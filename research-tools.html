RESEARCH UTILITIES / FIELD TOOLS

Tools for the work behind the paper.

Browser-based research utilities for exploratory data checks, journal shortlist management, reviewer responses, and manuscript structure. Your inputs stay in this browser unless you export them.

Data analysis starter

Upload a CSV with a header row. This tool computes descriptive statistics for numeric columns and pairwise Pearson correlations. It does not replace a validated statistical workflow; missing/non-numeric cells are excluded per column.
Choose a CSV file to begin. Data is processed in your browser.

Journal shortlist builder

Create and compare a shortlist from journal information you supply. No indexing, APC, quartile, or acceptance claims are inferred. Verify every journal detail with the publisher and indexing database before submission.

Reviewer response matrix

Track each reviewer comment, your response, and the manuscript change. The tool exports your entered content; it does not invent responses or claim a revision has been made.

Manuscript structure workspace

Extract text from TXT or DOCX to count words and inspect common section headings and citation/caption patterns. This is a heuristic checklist, not scientific review, plagiarism detection, or full cross-reference validation.
Upload a document to inspect its extracted text.
Privacy: this page runs analysis in your browser. It does not upload selected files. Export files are generated locally.
