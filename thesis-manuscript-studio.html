Project-specific checks

A thesis and a journal article have different requirements. Confirm the current institutional handbook or journal instructions before submission.
  • Research question, objectives and contribution are explicit.
  • Methods, units, data sources and exclusions are reproducible.
  • Results distinguish observation from interpretation.
  • Every figure and table is numbered, captioned and cited in text.
  • Claims match the evidence; limitations and uncertainty are reported.
  • References, DOI metadata and in-text citations have been verified.
  • Permissions, ethics, funding, conflicts and author contributions are addressed where applicable.
  • Abstract, keywords, word count and required declarations match the target format.
