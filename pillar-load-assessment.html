Pillar Load & Strength Assessment

Transparent tributary-area screening for individually scheduled pillars

Engineering scope: this tool performs a transparent, first-pass tributary-area calculation for user-defined rectangular tributary cells. It is not a numerical solver and does not infer tributary domains from arbitrary irregular geometry. Enter or import the tributary-cell dimensions for each pillar explicitly. Check stress assumptions, boundary conditions, load transfer, discontinuities, extraction sequence and mine-specific evidence before engineering use.

Equations and data provenance

Vertical stress: σv = γ × z, where γ is unit weight (MN/m³) and z is overburden depth (m), giving MPa.
Tributary-area pillar stress: σp = σv × (At / Ap), where At is the explicitly assigned tributary-cell area and Ap = pillar width × pillar length. The formula assumes the full load from At is carried by that pillar and does not resolve load sharing in irregular geometries.
Lunder–Pakalnis estimate: Sp = 0.44 UCS (0.68 + 0.52κ), with Cpav = 0.46 [log10(W/H + 0.75)]^[1.4/(W/H)] and κ = tan(acos((1 − Cpav)/(1 + Cpav))). Model implementation is constrained to W/H > 0.25; values outside its implemented domain are not estimated.
Krauland–Soder estimate: Sp = 0.354 UCS (0.778 + 0.222 W/H). Both empirical equations are shown for comparative screening only; neither should be treated as validated for a site without independent evidence.
